# DECISIONS.md

1. **Indexes.** I created a unique index on `emp_code` in the `employees` collection to strictly enforce uniqueness of employee codes and support fast point queries. I also created a compound unique index on `(emp_code, date)` in `attendance_logs` to efficiently prevent double punch-ins on the same day and to query specific employee records quickly. Additionally, I added an index on `date` in `attendance_logs` (descending) to optimize the `list_attendance` queries and trend endpoints, and an index on `department` in `employees`. I rejected creating an index on `status` since it has low cardinality.
2. **Punch-in race.** When two identical punch-in requests arrive simultaneously, they both evaluate the logic and attempt to insert. The first one succeeds, while the second hits the MongoDB unique index constraint on `(emp_code, date)`, throwing a `DuplicateKeyError`. My code catches this exception and returns a `409 Conflict`, effectively resolving the race condition without data duplication.
3. **Ties.** If two employees tie on late minutes, the MongoDB `$setWindowFields` with `$rank` assigns them the same competition rank (e.g., 1, 2, 2, 4). The leaderboard returns both if their rank is `<= limit`. They will then be sorted secondarily by `emp_code` ascending, providing deterministic output.
4. **Headcount.** The department summary starts by querying the `employees` collection and only `$lookup`s the `attendance_logs`. Since all employees in the department are passed through the pipeline regardless of whether they have matched logs or not, employees with zero logs contribute to the headcount via a `$sum: 1` grouping.
5. **One thing you would change.** If this had to serve 100x the data, I would introduce a pre-aggregated daily/monthly collection (or materialized view) for analytics. Computing aggregations on the fly across tens of millions of records (especially with `$densify` and full collection `$lookup`s) will degrade performance. Scheduled jobs or change streams could update the summary collections asynchronously.
